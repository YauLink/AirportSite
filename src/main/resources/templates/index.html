Before You Fly

🕒 Arrive Early

Domestic flights: arrive at least 2 hours before departure. International flights: arrive at least 3 hours before departure.

🛂 Travel Documents

Keep your passport or ID, boarding pass, and travel documents ready before entering the security checkpoint.

🛄 Baggage

Verify baggage allowance before departure to avoid excess baggage fees at the airport.

📱 Online Check-In

Check in online when available to save time and select your preferred seat before arriving.
